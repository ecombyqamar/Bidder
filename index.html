05 — How we evaluate opportunities

Not every job deserves an application.

Before any proposal is written, each listing is read against five questions. The answers help decide where effort goes, and where it does not.

  1. 1

    Skill Relevance

    Does the project align with the service provider's actual skills and experience?

  2. 2

    Project Clarity

    Are the scope, requirements, and expected deliverables understandable?

  3. 3

    Budget Suitability

    Is the stated or apparent budget appropriate for the work?

  4. 4

    Client Context

    Is there enough available information to make an informed decision about the opportunity?

  5. 5

    Proposal Potential

    Can the applicant offer a relevant, credible, and specific response?

From answers to a decision

07 — Working principles

Good work starts with a thoughtful process.

Six principles shape how the service is run day to day. They are working commitments, not promises of particular results.

  1. I

    Relevance Before Volume

    Focus on suitable opportunities rather than indiscriminate submissions.

  2. II

    Personalization Over Templates

    Respond to the project requirements instead of sending generic copy-paste messages.

  3. III

    Clear Communication

    Keep expectations, application status, and next steps understandable.

  4. IV

    Responsible Account Practices

    Respect account permissions, platform requirements, confidentiality, and applicable Upwork rules.

  5. V

    Continuous Improvement

    Use available information and feedback to refine the working process.

  6. VI

    Professional Accountability

    Keep records organized and communicate honestly about progress, limitations, and outcomes.

Reliable working relationships are built through consistency and transparency, one clearly recorded step at a time.
